# The Anthropology of Machines

**Mechanistic Interpretability for Social Scientists** *(working title)*

A book by Yunus Emre Tapan.

## What this is

Language models as an **object of study** rather than a tool: what they represent, how to
look inside them, and what claims that looking licenses. Written for social scientists —
people trained in construct validity, measurement, and reliability, who already have the
instincts this field needs.

## Status

A working draft, written in the open. The preface is up; chapters follow as they become
readable. Read it at
[yemretapan.com/anthropology-of-machines](https://yemretapan.com/anthropology-of-machines).

## How this book is written

I write the manuscript. I use AI as an assistant — for copy-editing, for checking technical
details against sources, and for running the tooling that builds and publishes the site — not
to write the prose. The book is drafted in public, chapter by chapter, and the commit history
reflects that.
